# Recorded samples

The files in this folder are edited, trimmed, looped and packed versions of recordings from two public-domain sample libraries by Versilian Studios:

- **VS Chamber Orchestra: Community Edition (VSCO 2 CE)**, https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE, recorded by Sam Gossner and Simon Dalzell, sample cutting by Elan Hickler (Soundemote). License: CC0 1.0 Universal.
  Used for: violin, viola and cello sections, contrabass, violin pizzicato, harp, flute, oboe, clarinet, trumpet, French horn, tenor trombone, timpani.
- **Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL)**, https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL. License: CC0 1.0 Universal.
  Used for: Steinway B grand piano, tenor saxophone, vibraphone, marimba, glockenspiel, xylophone, tubular bells, mbira, and the percussion bank (claps, hi-hat, shaker, tambourine, conga, bongos, cowbell, claves, woodblock, triangle, cymbals, gong, snare, tom, bass drum, darbuka, frame drum, cajon, cabasa, agogo, guiro, sleigh bells, mark tree).

CC0 places these recordings in the public domain: they can be used, changed and shared for any purpose, including commercial use, without asking permission. Credit is not required, but we give it gladly.

Each `.mp3` holds every sample of one instrument back to back after a short sync click; `index.json` lists each sample's position, root note, velocity layer, tuning correction and loop points.
